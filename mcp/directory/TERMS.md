# Terms of use for OPDIS Contributors

You can use the contributor tools to explore public OPDIS research, inspect the evidence linked by the project and prepare draft contributions. You remain in control of whether to publish a draft.

The project distributes the contributor software under the [MIT License](../../LICENSE). That license sets out the permissions, copyright notice and warranty disclaimer for the software. If you submit material to the project, follow the [contributor guide](../../CONTRIBUTING.md). It asks for original contributions under the project's MIT terms and for the origin and permissible license of any third-party material. Preparing a private draft with these tools does not submit it to the project.

The project tracker and experimental findings describe the recorded state of a research prototype. Check the linked evidence and open discussions before relying on a result or claiming a task.
