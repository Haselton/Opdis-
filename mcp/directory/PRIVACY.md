# OPDIS Contributors privacy policy

The OPDIS Contributors service helps people find public research tasks, inspect project evidence and prepare example data for review.

**Information used by each tool.** Finding a task uses the equipment or role you select. The evidence tool requires no input. When you prepare test cases, your MCP client sends the public handle, task description, scoring rationale, example identifiers, prompts and expected answers you provide. The service checks these in memory and sends the result back to your client. A pseudonym is enough.

**Storage and sharing.** The OPDIS application has no user database, advertising trackers or draft-content logs. It does not send your draft to GitHub, other contributors or volunteer GPUs. The hosting platform processes network requests, and your chat provider may retain the request and reply under its own policies. The public site's browser-only draft form runs on your device. If you choose to publish a draft on GitHub, the material you post is public and subject to GitHub's policies.

**Your choices.** Use original, shareable examples and leave out passwords and personal records. You decide whether to save your draft or post it elsewhere. Use the controls of the service where you saved or posted a copy to manage that copy.

For privacy questions, [contact the project maintainers](https://github.com/Haselton/Opdis-/issues). GitHub issues are public. Ask for a private contact channel if needed and leave personal details out of the issue.
