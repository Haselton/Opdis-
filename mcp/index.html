OPDISGitHub project

Help test an AI that learns to improve.

OPDIS is an open-source project building toward a shared GPU network. You can contribute research cases from your phone or help measure what happens when a second GPU joins.

Contribute from your phone

Write four original questions with exact answers. Two teach the task and two check whether the instruction works on new inputs. A second task family helps test whether an improvement carries over.

Follow the evidence

The published baseline demonstrated task sharing on two simulated workers. A physical two-GPU speedup and sustained improvement on unfamiliar tasks are open research questions.
